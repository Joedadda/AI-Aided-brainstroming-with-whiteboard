# Dabba Weekly: pause and cancel review (fictional)

Attendees: Ritu (product), Joel (engineering), Sana (support), Farhan (design)

## What we looked at
- About 3 in 10 subscribers pause their lunch plan in the second week, and half of those never come back.
- Support gets around 40 tickets a week asking how to skip a single day instead of pausing the whole plan.
- The pause button sits three screens deep, under Account, then Plan, then Manage.
- Joel said skipping one day is a small backend change because deliveries are already planned per day.

## Ideas raised
- Add a "Skip tomorrow" button on the home screen.
- Send a message the evening before with the next day's menu and a skip link.
- Let people swap a dish instead of skipping the day.
- Farhan suggested showing how many meals are left this week on the home screen.

## Agreed
- Ship "Skip tomorrow" first, behind a test with 10 percent of users.
- Sana will tag every pause ticket for two weeks so we know the reasons.

## Open questions
- Do people pause because of the menu, the price or the delivery time?
- Will a skip button reduce pauses, or just move them?
- Should skipped days roll over or be refunded?
- Who owns the evening message: product or the kitchen team?
