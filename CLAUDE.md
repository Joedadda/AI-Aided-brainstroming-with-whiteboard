# Rules for every session

1. **Update `PROGRESS.md` at the end of every turn.** No exceptions, including turns that only read or answer a question. Log what was asked, what changed (files, decisions), what was checked and what was not, and keep the "Last updated" line and the "Next steps" list current. A new session must be able to pick up from it without anything else.
2. Read `PROGRESS.md` first, then `Build_PRD-1.md` (the build spec), then `project_context.md` (background).
3. User-facing copy: plain language, no jargon, dry tone, no em dashes, no emojis.
4. Check clicks with real pointer events, not `element.click()`.
